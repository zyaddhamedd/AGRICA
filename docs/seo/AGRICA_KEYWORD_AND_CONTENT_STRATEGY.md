# AGRICA keyword and content strategy

Date: 2026-11-03. Inventory-led strategy, not a keyword-volume or ranking report. Counts: 34 Produce products (21 fresh, 13 frozen), 27 Herbs products in six families. Existing product names and navigation are translated in EN/AR/RU/DE/FR. All demand/priority labels below are hypotheses pending Search Console and buyer evidence.

## Intent-to-page map

| Buyer intent hypothesis | Primary target | Content basis / boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Egyptian agricultural exporters; bulk agricultural products from Egypt | `/{lang}`; English `/en/company`; `/contact` | Existing export/company identity. Do not create five port/market doorway pages. |
| Egyptian fresh produce suppliers | `/{lang}/products` and visible fresh directory | Approved inventory and factual product records; query filter is not a separate canonical landing page. |
| Frozen vegetables suppliers Egypt | Same catalogue with frozen links, IQF broccoli pilot | Existing frozen inventory and approved broccoli cut/pack/storage facts; no fabricated availability. |
| Import Egyptian oranges; Egyptian oranges supplier | `/{lang}/products/oranges` | Approved distinct varieties, season, calibre, packing and conditional handling. |
| Egyptian fresh strawberries suppliers | `/{lang}/products/fresh-strawberries` | Approved fresh record, not IQF duplicate. |
| Egyptian frozen strawberries suppliers; IQF strawberry bulk enquiry | `/{lang}/products/iqf-strawberries` | Approved frozen record and genuine contact path. |
| Egyptian herbs and spices exporters | `/{lang}/herbs-spices` and `/herbs-spices/products` | Source-backed identity/families; no product-level process/certificate assumptions. |
| Hibiscus/chamomile/basil wholesale supplier | Existing Herbs catalogue/family discovery | Need approved detailed product briefs before separate indexable pages. |

The four pilots are intentionally bounded. Other 29 approved Produce records can be considered after evidence of buyer demand and reviewed translations; fries remains blocked. Use one page per distinct product, not per synonym/variety/import port. No invented MOQ, pricing, shipping terms, compliance or real-time stock.

## Competitor observations

[AGROEGYPT](https://agroegypt.com/brands/): clear citrus/variety/product navigation. [Alliance Foods](https://alliance-foods.co/en/products/fresh-produce): product sections, packaging/calibre context and quote CTA. [Fiora](https://fiora-eg.com/): named Herbs/flowers/seeds access. These are observed architecture patterns, not rankings, traffic, market share or validated opportunities. AGRICA uses its own approved source material only.

## Qualified enquiry path and measurement

Discovery → useful product/spec page → email or standalone contact; catalogue selection remains available. Forms explicitly preview and must not emit “lead sent” claims. Owner should connect an approved delivery workflow, consent-aware analytics and validated success event before counting leads. Track qualified importer enquiries and destination/product relevance rather than raw visits.

## Business/content requirements

1. Approve fries facts; retain internal review flags until then.
2. Confirm per-product Herbs forms, quality parameters, packing, sample workflow and available documentation; certify nothing by inference.
3. Commission full Company translations and native review of technical values; then consider self-canonical Company locale pages and hreflang.
4. Verify current product availability, commercial terms and actual market/port capabilities before claiming them.
5. Verify enquiry delivery/ownership and privacy handling before enabling real submission.
6. Collect Search Console query/page/country and field CWV data, sales questions and lead qualification evidence.
7. Maintain first-party source ownership and evidence dates. Do not change sitemap lastmod unless page content actually changed; do not imply stock updates from a build time.

## 30 / 60 / 90 days

- 30: approved deployment, production crawl/redirect checks, Search Console ownership/sitemap submission, inspect representative URLs, verify enquiry routing and native metadata/UI review.
- 60: use actual queries and qualified enquiries to improve the four pilot pages, translate Company/technical details, commission a small set of detailed Herbs briefs; resolve unapproved fries source and existing trust-copy issues.
- 90: expand only evidence-backed products/categories; compare page-country-language conversions and field CWV; editorial/link partnerships based on real business relationships, never backlink spam or duplicated market pages.

AI discovery uses the same crawlable, factual, linked content and standard schema foundations. No guarantee of AI citations, Google rankings, indexing or traffic is made.
